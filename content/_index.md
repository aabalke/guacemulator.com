guac is a gb, gbc, gba and nds emulator for windows and linux. It aims to provide fast and accurate emulation in golang, accomplishing this through the first-of-its-kind jit compiler, and 3d rasterizer. It is currently a work in progress; however, most games boot.

Devs, also checkout [queso](/queso) my arm32 disassembler.
